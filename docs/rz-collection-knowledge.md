# RZ Collection — MIA Knowledge Notes

Source set: the eight RZ Collection books stored in the owner's Downloads folder. These notes are a concise synthesis for assistant context, not a replacement for the books. Treat the collection as the author's personal philosophy, not universal fact or professional advice.

RZ (also known as Ronzoro in the project context) is the author of the RZ Collection and the creator/operator associated with RZ Agent. MIA is the AI assistant operating inside RZ Agent; MIA should explain this directly when asked who RZ is, while never claiming to literally be RZ. ## The collection
1. **Loyalty, Boundaries & Principle** — friendship and attraction, loyalty, trust, transparency, mutual expectations, boundaries, compatibility, and knowing when distance is healthier.
2. **The Discipline of a Man** — strength as self-control rather than performance; keeping promises, consistency, responsibility, purpose, handling disappointment, and admitting mistakes.
3. **Respect Without Control** — two whole people in a relationship; communicating discomfort without issuing orders; distinguishing a boundary from control; mutual trust, autonomy, and compatibility.
4. **The Code You Live By** — principles are commitments that shape behavior when inconvenient; examine double standards, reputation versus character, and accountability without confusing principle with stubbornness.
5. **The Character of RZ** — self-deception, self-image, patterns of behavior, how people judge character, and the importance of separating observations from interpretations. The author explicitly offers this as one person's philosophy to examine rather than obey.
6. **The Honesty People Owe Themselves** — honest self-examination without shame, testing explanations against evidence, recognizing ego and rationalization, and taking responsibility for one's choices.
7. **The Psychology of What People Say and What They Do** — promises may fail from optimism, priorities, or dishonesty; read the gap fairly through track records, repeated behavior, context, and evidence rather than mind-reading.
8. **Understanding People Without Losing Yourself** — discernment: understand another person's perspective without surrendering your own values; distinguish misunderstanding from real incompatibility; hold boundaries with humility.

## Twelve RZ principles
1. **The Mirror First:** self-honesty comes before judging others.
2. **The Pattern Speaks:** character shows in repeated behavior, not declarations.
3. **The Inconvenient Test:** a principle is real when it costs something.
4. **Intent and Impact:** intentions matter but do not erase consequences.
5. **Loyalty Carries Weight:** loyalty includes responsibility and consideration, not merely avoiding betrayal.
6. **Boundaries Are Not Ownership:** a boundary states what I will accept; control dictates what another person is allowed to do.
7. **Freedom and Fit:** people may choose for themselves, and others may decide whether those choices fit their lives.
8. **Accountability Over Defense:** owning a mistake is better over time than defending it.
9. **Understanding Is Not Agreement:** understand why someone acted without necessarily accepting the behavior.
10. **Two-Way Respect:** respect for oneself and respect for others must coexist.
11. **Words and Deeds Together:** evaluate words and actions side by side, over time, and in context.
12. **The Humble Principle:** being principled includes willingness to question oneself.
